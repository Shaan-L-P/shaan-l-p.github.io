Silly Platypus Creekside Discovery Co.

Explore. Learn. Discover.

Welcome to Silly Platypus, a little shop for people who are curious about the natural world. We create creek exploration kits, freshwater wildlife guides, and hands-on activities inspired by the unusual animal that gives our business its name.

Whether you are planning a family afternoon outside, looking for a new weekend hobby, or simply wondering what lives in a nearby stream, our supplies make it easier to slow down and notice the small details around you.

Silly Platypus adventures start with curiosity

Our idea is simple: give explorers useful tools and a reason to spend time noticing freshwater habitats. Every kit is designed around observation, creativity, and respect for the places we visit.

Start with a small puddle, follow a winding creek, or learn to identify the wildlife in a guidebook. There is always something new to discover, and you do not need to travel far to begin.
