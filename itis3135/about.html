Where This Is All Headed

By this point in the semester I've built out three separate but connected corners of the web, each one serving a different purpose but all tied back together through links. Rather than cramming everything into one page, the goal has been to keep personal, academic, and professional content cleanly separated while still making it easy to move between them.

This course site in particular exists to document that process. It's where my coursework, assignments, and reflections for ITIS 3135 live, and it links out to the other two pieces of the puzzle so anyone visiting can see how the whole structure fits together.

  • Personal Home Page: Sits at the root of my webspace account and works as my general landing page and portfolio, separate from any single class.
  • Course Site: Lives inside the itis3135 folder (the site you're looking at right now) and holds everything built specifically for this class, from my introduction to this about page.
  • Design Firm Site: A separate site inside its own folder, built to look and feel like a real freelance design business rather than a class assignment, with its own distinct branding and layout.
