Platypus Agent Trading Cards

Meet a team of unusual platypus agents! Each agent has a unique personality, special abilities, and an important mission. Collect them all and discover what makes each one special.

Agent P

Codename: The Silent Operative

Agent P is a highly skilled secret agent who completes dangerous missions without saying a word. His calm attitude and clever strategies make him a reliable member of the team.

Special Ability: Stealth and infiltration

Agent Rank: Elite

Detective Platypus

Codename: The Truth Seeker

Detective Platypus specializes in solving difficult mysteries. With sharp instincts and an eye for detail, no clue is too small and no case is too complicated.

Special Ability: Investigation and deduction

Agent Rank: Master Detective

Turbo Platypus

Codename: The Speedster

Turbo Platypus moves faster than almost anyone can follow. Whether racing against time or escaping a dangerous situation, this agent always knows how to make a quick getaway.

Special Ability: Super speed

Agent Rank: Advanced

Wizard Platypus

Codename: The Arcane Expert

Wizard Platypus uses mysterious magic to protect the team and overcome impossible challenges. His knowledge of spells makes him especially useful when ordinary strategies fail.

Special Ability: Spellcasting and enchantments

Agent Rank: Arcane Master

Super Platypus

Codename: The Mighty Protector

Super Platypus uses incredible strength and courage to defend others. When the team faces its toughest challenge, this hero steps forward and refuses to give up.

Special Ability: Super strength

Agent Rank: Hero Class

Explorer Platypus

Codename: The Trailblazer

Explorer Platypus travels into unfamiliar places to discover hidden secrets. His resourcefulness and adventurous spirit help the team navigate strange environments and find new paths.

Special Ability: Exploration and survival

Agent Rank: Pathfinder
